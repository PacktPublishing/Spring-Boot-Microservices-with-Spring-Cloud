Quick Note!
If you are seeing a red 'x' sign in pom.xml, this is due to known bug.

It does not have any impact in terms on building/running the project, etc. But if don't want to see that red 'x' anymore, you can fix it this way..

Thanks to Ninos (Student) for trying to find solution..



for what appears the 'x' error in pom.xml because of version

<version>2.1.5.RELEASE</version>

, here is the solution :

1- pom.xml add to the properties:

<properties>

<java.version>1.8</java.version>

<maven-jar-plugin.version>3.1.1</maven-jar-plugin.version>

</properties>

2- update maven project:

right click to the project ---> Maven ---> Update Project
